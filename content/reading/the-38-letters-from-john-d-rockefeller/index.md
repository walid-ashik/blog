## My highlights so far

### On action

- **Do the hard thing first.** The more difficult or unpleasant a task is, the sooner you should start it. Waiting only makes it harder and scarier, like aiming a gun for so long that you never pull the trigger.
- **There's never enough time.** There will always be more to do than time to do it. Worrying about all of it only freezes you, so pick the work that matters and finish it. *(p. 20)*
- **"Later" means "never".** Saying tomorrow, next week or someday is the same as saying it won't get done. Stop waiting for perfect conditions and start now. *(p. 21)*
- **Ideas are cheap. Doing them is rare.** The world is full of people with ideas but short on people who can carry a good one through. One idea put into action beats a thousand thought up at home. *(p. 21)*

### On failure and hardship

- **Same situation, different eyes.** Optimists find opportunity in suffering. Pessimists find suffering in opportunity. His formula for success: Dream + Failure + Challenge. *(p. 34)*
- **Never struggling is the real bad luck.** Hardship builds character and the ability to get things done. People who have never faced misfortune are the unfortunate ones. *(p. 64)*

### On people and leadership

- **You can't do big things alone.** Big achievements depend on other people's strength. To lead people well, first do the small jobs yourself so you know what it feels like to be the one taking orders. *(p. 64)*
- **Two kinds of smart people.** Some succeed by using their own talent: artists, scholars, actors. Others succeed by bringing out the talent in other people: managers and leaders. *(pp. 64–65)*

### On humility and decisions

- **Play dumb on purpose.** The smarter you are, the more it pays to stay humble and hide how clever you are, the way ripe rice bends lower the heavier it gets.
- **"Let me wait before talking."** His rule in business: think calmly before deciding, and once you've decided, act without hesitation.
